# v2.2.3

Flight boards use the locally bundled Doto font, distributed under its included SIL Open Font License. Trip rows align time, route and status. A scheduled flight becomes delayed at its departure time and cancels automatically after three hours without takeoff. Opening preflight writes `takeoffAt: null`; only an actual takeoff starts the running trip status. This also corrects older preflight records when matching local active-run evidence exists.

The global companion evaluates reminders every 15 seconds, on focus and on visibility changes. Milestones are one hour before (check-in closed), 30 minutes before (crew, close doors), departure, 25 minutes after (delayed/disembark), and three hours after (cancelled). Only the latest relevant milestone is replayed after a suspended session. Keys include the account, booking, scheduled time and stage. Flights that have departed, completed or been manually cancelled do not receive missed-departure warnings. Browser notifications require permission and an open application; this release does not provision a push service capable of waking a closed browser.

The sampling registry additionally parses the public-domain OurAirports dataset, including small airfields and heliports and excluding closed facilities. It reads explicit latitude/longitude fields and de-duplicates nearby-identical coordinates. Sampling remains bounded, rotating and dependent on provider coverage. No new commercial airports or routes are created from that registry.

Brasileirinho is a separate special profile, searchable by name and PS-BZR. The itinerary is supplied by the user through 2026-10-30; representative airport choices are not independently verified historical movements. The single-aircraft profile has no invented IATA/ICAO airline code or flight number. Only a matching PS-BZR registration/callsign can appear in its live panel. Historical legs are never promoted to live flights or used to guess its next destination. The selected leg can be simulated using the Bonanza or a Cessna substitution; live tracking continues to target PS-BZR. Its representative airfields do not enter the commercial airport database.

The separate `myroster-observed-route-numbers` IndexedDB database retains up to 20,000 operator/direction/number identities for 180 days, learned only from fresh airborne radar observations. It records observed aircraft types and registrations. This local reference catalog survives live-position expiry but does not imply an aircraft is airborne now; route suggestions show observation dates. No passenger numbers are inferred from alphanumeric operational callsigns or registrations. Shared persistence still depends on the existing configured server history service.

Estimated arrivals are anchored to the observation timestamp and remaining great-circle distance, using plausible measured ground speed or a labelled aircraft-speed estimate. Timetable arrival estimates take precedence; missing timetable arrivals can use departed-at plus route distance. Refreshing a lost position does not reset its observation time or slide its ETA forward. Transatlantic labels identify Americas–other-region great-circle routes that pass through the Atlantic corridor, including NBJ–GRU; they are route classifications, not confirmation of current airborne position. Unknown destinations show arrival unknown. Geographic last-seen descriptions use a nearby reference airport or coordinates; timetable-only data shows when it was checked instead of fabricating a last-seen position.
